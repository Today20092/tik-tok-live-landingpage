# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Live-stream viewers, seekers, and new Muslims looking for trustworthy Quran resources. This includes non-Muslims requesting an English translation and readers continuing their learning after a live session.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors find Quran resources and take a clear next step: read a translation, request an eligible free copy, find the physical edition used during Ayoub's live sessions, or continue learning. Articles and learning videos support that primary journey.

The homepage journey prioritizes finding the live books, then Quran resources, then articles, with donation last.

## Operating Context

Ayoub shares the site with viewers of his live Quran reading sessions. Visitors can use the resource hub independently and reach him through his existing public social profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve resource links, articles, learning videos, and public social profiles.
- State country, audience, and quantity restrictions on free-copy offers accurately.
- Preserve the static Cloudflare Pages deployment and production domain, `https://islam.ayoubabed.xyz`.
- Follow AGENTS.md for implementation, metadata, and religious-source requirements. Follow the linked article guides when working on Quran or hadith content.
- Use the existing design system documented in `docs/lumos-design-system.md` for refinements.

## Brand Commitments

Use clear, direct, accurate copy suitable for people with different levels of familiarity with Islam. Preserve the quiet, compact resource-hub experience. Keep support invitations voluntary and claims verifiable.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing resource content, articles, learning videos, and public profiles are the source material. Verify destinations and source quotations when changing their content; invent no contact details, endorsements, or eligibility promises.

## Product Principles

- Make trustworthy resources easy to find.
- Explain eligibility before visitors follow an offer.
- Support learning without assuming prior religious knowledge.
- Keep the core resource journey clear as supporting content grows.
