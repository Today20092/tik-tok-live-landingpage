Assalamu alaikum, and welcome! I'm Ayoub. If you've joined one of my live Quran readings or you're visiting for the first time, I'm glad you're here.

I put this page together so you can find the resources we talk about during the live. You can read an English translation, find the edition I use, or catch up on recordings.

If you'd like a free English copy, the links explain who can request one. Eligibility and delivery depend on the provider.

You don't need to know where to start or have everything figured out. Whether you're Muslim, new to Islam, or simply curious about the Quran, you're welcome to read along.

Head back to the main page and choose a link that interests you. I hope to see you in a live session soon.

Ayoub
