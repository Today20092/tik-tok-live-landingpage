## Start with one question

Give each article a clear subject. Explain the main point, then add the context a reader needs to understand it.

- Keep paragraphs focused on one idea.
- Link to sources near the claims they support.
- Distinguish your own reflections from quotations.

### Add references

You can link to [Quran.com](https://quran.com) or another relevant source using ordinary Markdown links.

## Organize your notes

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Question | What the reader wants to understand |
| Explanation | Your main point and supporting context |
| Sources | References the reader can follow |
