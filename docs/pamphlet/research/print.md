## Artwork specification

- Use a landscape **11 × 8.5 in flat trim**, **letter/C-fold**, and **4/4 full color**. Folded size is about **3.69 × 8.5 in**. Start with **100 lb. matte text** for a text-heavy handout and price **100 lb. gloss text** alongside it.
- Use **0.125 in bleed on all four outer edges**, for a **11.25 × 8.75 in bleed area**. Extend backgrounds through the bleed and keep text, logos and QR codes at least **0.125 in from trim and folds**. Use images at **300 ppi at placed size**.
- Lay out two flat spreads, outside first and inside second. On the outside, left to right, place the tuck-in flap, back cover and front cover. On the inside, the short flap is on the right.
- The U.S. Press guide uses outside panel widths **3.625 / 3.6875 / 3.6875 in** (folds at **3.625 and 7.3125 in** from left trim), and inside widths **3.6875 / 3.6875 / 3.625 in** (folds at **3.6875 and 7.375 in**). These are the guide's dimensions. Use the selected printer's template for production, especially if it lists a different folded width.
- Export a print PDF with fonts embedded and guide layers hidden. Follow the selected printer's color profile, crop-mark and upload settings. Request a PDF proof and fold a physical mock-up to check panel order and QR placement before approval.
