# Font pairings for Live with Ayoub

Researched 11 September 2026. The owner likes DM Sans and Manrope headings with Source Sans 3 body text, and dislikes Geist. The shortlist extends those preferences rather than replacing the site's quiet, compact feel.

## Suggested comparisons

These are design recommendations, not objectively ranked or scientifically proven pairings. The primary sources below establish each typeface's intended use; the combinations and descriptions are my judgment for this site. Names are **headings + body and controls**.

| Pairing | Why try it here |
| --- | --- |
| **Manrope + Source Sans 3** | Keep the existing favorite as the benchmark: distinct headings and understated reading text. |
| **Manrope + DM Sans** | My first new option. Keeps the preferred heading character and introduces the other preferred font throughout the links and paragraphs. |
| **DM Sans + Source Sans 3** | A quieter all-sans alternative. Less visual separation than a serif pairing; compare the banner and article title carefully. |
| **Newsreader + DM Sans** | My first serif option. Adds a bookish heading voice while keeping controls and paragraphs familiar. |
| **Lora + Source Sans 3** | More calligraphic personality in headings, balanced by plain reading text. Check the compact homepage as well as articles. |
| **Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3** | The most directly supported family pairing: Adobe explicitly designed Source Serif to complement Source Sans. A restrained editorial alternative. |

Keep DM Sans alone as a reference. Remove Geist from the comparison because the owner has rejected it. Three serif trials are enough to establish whether that direction suits the site; avoid a large unrelated font catalogue.

## What the primary sources establish

- **DM Sans:** Google Fonts describes a geometric sans with low stroke contrast, intended for smaller text, with a Latin Extended character set. This supports trying it for the site's compact links and English body text. [Google Fonts description](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html)
- **Manrope:** Google Fonts identifies it as a modern open-source sans designed by Mikhail Sharanda. Its suitability here is also grounded in the owner's existing preference. [Google Fonts description](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/DESCRIPTION.en_us.html)
- **Source Sans 3:** Adobe designed the family for user-interface environments. It is therefore a reasonable common body/control font for comparisons. [Adobe repository](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans)
- **Newsreader:** Production Type designed this open-source serif for longer on-screen reading, with optical sizes and display cuts. Using it for headings is a restrained way to test its personality without changing every paragraph. [Production Type](https://productiontype.com/font/newsreader)
- **Lora:** Cyreal describes a contemporary, moderately contrasted text serif with calligraphic roots, optimized for screens. [Cyreal](https://www.cyreal.org/fonts/lora/)
- **Source Serif 4:** Adobe explicitly identifies Source Serif as a complement to Source Sans. This is the strongest first-party evidence for a deliberate pairing in the shortlist. [Adobe repository](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif)

## How to judge the preview

Use the same content, colors, sizes, and spacing while switching. Compare the portrait heading, longest link title, welcome article paragraphs, and podcast notes at desktop and phone widths. Prefer the option that remains comfortable and fits the site's voice across all of them. Typeface choice alone does not establish accessibility.

Apply the heading face to titles and section headings; keep navigation, buttons, labels, and prose in the body face. Keep these comparisons development-only. After choosing, retain only the selected production font files and needed weights.
