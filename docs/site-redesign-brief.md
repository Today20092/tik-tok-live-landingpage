## Purpose and audience

Serve visitors arriving from Ayoub's live Quran reading sessions. Help them recognize and find the books mentioned on live, with online Quran reading and copy options nearby. The current concern is a lack of professional finish and consistency across the site.

## Confirmed decisions

- Make finding the live books the clearest opening task.
- Keep both live books and the main Quran options visible; reserve disclosures for secondary details and extra resources.
- Keep articles, videos, and other learning resources discoverable with less homepage emphasis than the live books.
- Keep articles in a simple chronological list. Do not add topic filters.
- Make Ayoub's identity visible as the curator while the resources lead.
- Standardize typography, spacing, navigation, buttons, cards, and controls across public pages. Use distinct layouts where the task warrants them, especially article reading.
- Remove repeated explanations and reorganize or demote competing content when that improves the main journeys; preserve useful resources and factual meaning.
- Allow substantial layout and typography changes while retaining a quiet, trustworthy, reading-friendly character.

## Scope and constraints

Review the homepage, shared navigation and footer, article index and articles, search, and recovery pages. Classify alternate layouts and development previews before including them in the public redesign scope.

Reuse the pinned Lumos foundation and shared components. Preserve religious-source requirements, accurate eligibility disclosures, public social destinations, metadata, static hosting, light and dark themes, and accessibility. Support invitations remain secondary and voluntary.

Verify that a new live-stream visitor can recognize the books and reach the appropriate resource without explanation. Inspect consuming pages on desktop and narrow screens, in both themes, with enlarged text and keyboard focus. Verify search loading, empty, failure, and no-JavaScript states.
